Moko, the little cloud who wanted to rain.

Moko was a little cloud, floating over a very dry garden.

Down below, the flowers hung their heads and waited.

Moko puffed and puffed, but not one drop came.

Moko felt a little sad, and let out a tiny sigh.

Then, softly, soft little drops began to fall.

Pitter, patter, pitter, patter. The garden drank it up.

And the flowers opened, one by one, to say thank you.

Good night, little Moko.

The End
