Ever wonder how a rainbow forms?
It starts with sunlight and
a lot of tiny raindrops.
Sunlight looks white,
but it is secretly a
mix of every color.
When a ray enters a raindrop,
it slows down and bends.
That bend is called refraction.
Inside,
the light bounces off
the back of the drop,
like a tiny mirror.
On the way out it bends again,
and the colors fan out.
Red bends the least,
and violet bends the most.
Each drop sends just one
color toward your eye.
Higher drops send you red,
and lower drops send you violet.
Millions of drops,
each sending its own color,
paint a bright arc across the sky.
Stand with the sun behind you,
and the rainbow appears in front.
Now you know how it forms!
