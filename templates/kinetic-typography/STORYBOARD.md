# Storyboard: kinetic-typography (20 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps, 120 BPM, silent)

Beat = 15 frames. 40 beats = 600 frames. Font Anton, caps. Words slam in (scale 1.3 to 1, 6 f, expo.out); the key word is accent colored, thumps on beats 3-6, exits (scale 0.9 + fade, 5 f) just before the next phrase.

| # | Frames | On-screen text | Visual + motion | Audio cue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-120 (beats 0-7) | SHIP / SMALL. | Black stage, white word hard-cut to orange key word at beat 2 | silent, beat grid only |
| 2 | 120-240 (beats 8-15) | SHIP / OFTEN. | Same layout, same anchor | silent |
| 3 | 240-360 (beats 16-23) | LISTEN / HARD. | Downbeat (4 bars): stage inverts to white, black type, blue key word | silent |
| 4 | 360-480 (beats 24-31) | FIX / FAST. | Same as 3 | silent |
| 5 | 480-600 (beats 32-39) | REPEAT. | Poster color-block: full-frame fields wipe in every beat (orange, black, blue, white, orange), word cropped at 131% width; final field zooms out to a fully legible word, held 4 beats (2 s) | silent |
