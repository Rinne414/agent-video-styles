# Storyboard: Prism Studio logo sting

8 s, 240 frames, 1920x1080, 30 fps, silent. One continuous shot, one hero object (glass prism). Frame ranges derive from the style's 6 s / 10 s structure, scaled to 8 s.

| # | Frames | On-screen text | Visual + motion | Audio cue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Void | 0-24 | none | Deep indigo void `#06081A`, 70 seeded dust motes drift, violet rim light fades up | silent |
| 2 Prism in | 6-66 | none | Glass prism scales 0.92 to 1.0 and fades in, slow Y rotation, camera orbit starts | silent |
| 3 Beam | 24-66 | none | White comet beam travels in from the left edge to the prism's left face | silent |
| 4 Hit | 66-92 | none | Bloom-style flash at the entry point (3 frames full, 22 frames decay), light appears inside the glass, a sheen sweeps across the prism | silent |
| 5 Spectrum sweep | 66-128 | none | Seven coloured bands leave the right face, fan opens to 26 degrees and sweeps from low to high across the frame | silent |
| 6 Recombine | 128-168 | none | Fan closes to one horizontal line, bands add back to white; prism slides left to its lockup position | silent |
| 7 Resolve | 146-192 | PRISM | A light front runs along the line left to right; letters ignite in spectrum colours, then cool to white; spectrum underline draws in | silent |
| 8 Lockup | 192-240 | PRISM | Prism drifts (0.1 deg per frame), a second sheen crosses it, wordmark held 1.5 s+ | silent |

Layout: prism at left, wordmark at right, both centred on the beam line (y = 540). Wordmark is DOM text (Inter 600, 170 px) so it stays crisp.
