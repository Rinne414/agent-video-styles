# Storyboard: painterly-canvas, "Night Rain" (20 s, 1080x1080, 30 fps, silent)

Style: painterly-canvas (watercolor washes + ink bristle strokes, procedural, seeded). Structure scaled from the 900-frame recipe to 600 frames. One subject throughout: a window, whose glass changes with each lyric line. Brush-stroke wipes (zigzag passes of a wide dry brush, about 22 frames) repaint the glass between stages.

| # | Frames | On-screen text | Visual + motion | Audio cue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Paper | 0-30 | none | Blank grained paper; ink window frame, sill and transom draw on (frames 4-56) with boiling line | silent |
| 1 | 30-150 | "Rain on the window" | Sky-blue wipe (from f24) and wash bloom; thin rain streaks fall, droplet beads appear; sky-colored brush band draws under the line, text revealed left to right | silent |
| 2 | 150-285 | "small rivers of light" | Teal wipe; six ochre rivulets draw down the glass with glowing heads, then slow light dots slide along them | silent |
| 3 | 285-420 | "the city goes soft" | Indigo wipe; building strokes rise from the sill, ochre and vermilion bokeh bloom, then grow and soften; rain turns pale | silent |
| 4 Climax | 420-530 | "and I stay the night" | Warm ochre wipe; vermilion lamp wash blooms past the glass edge; small sepia figure draws on (3 strokes) and boils at idle | silent |
| 5 Close | 530-600 | (line 4 holds) + credit "night rain" | Art washes fade to ~30% back into paper; credit fades in at f550; everything settled from f590, held about 0.5 s plus the fade tail | silent |

Camera: whole art layer zooms 0.7% per second around the window. Lyrics: stroke band draws on over 20 frames, text reveals over 24 frames with fade in plus 6 px rise (10 frames), fade out over 8 frames, rotation within +/-1 degree.
Boil: every stroke re-seeded each 3 frames, jitter at most 1.5 px. Grain: three seeded variants cycled every 3 frames plus a static paper mottle.
