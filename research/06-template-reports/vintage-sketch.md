## Review correction: draw-on was popping, not drawing
Coordinator reported empty paper under headlines in the MP4 at 9.4 s and 21.9 s. Art was present at other times (my contact sheet showed it), but at those times the plate was visibly empty for about 0.5-1 s, and I had not verified draw-on motion from the MP4.
- Root cause: GSAP's CSS plugin tweened `strokeDashoffset` as a style property and rounded it to whole pixels (`1px` -> `0px`). With `pathLength="1"` the property only has values 1 and 0, so every contour, hatch line and leader stayed invisible, then popped in fully at 50% of its tween. Confirmed in headless Chrome: inline `stroke-dashoffset` read `1px` at frames 655/660 and `0px` at 666. Snapshots matched the MP4 (same timeline), so the renderer was not at fault; the bug was in the authored tween. Contributing: contours used a slow `power2.inOut` start, and the colophon had text only for the first 0.5 s.
- Fix: tween the attribute, not the style: `{ attr: { "stroke-dashoffset": 1 } }` -> `{ attr: { "stroke-dashoffset": 0 } }`. Probe now reads 0.81, 0.61, 0.42, 0.14 at frames 655, 660, 666, 680. Also contours start at plate frame 1 with `power2.out`, headline and colophon text rise earlier, crossfade 6 frames.
- Verified from the final MP4 (ffmpeg frame extraction, viewed full size): 4.0 s (Fig. 1 gear/spring/weight partly drawn), 9.4 s (Fig. 2 wheel spokes and anchor arms mid-draw), 18.5 s (Fig. 3 pendulum swinging, hatching, leader mid-draw, note visible), 21.9 s (colophon icons mid-draw next to "Weight pushes."). Draw-on is now progressive. Earlier PASS items that relied on the old render still hold for the settled frames (checked again at 18.5 s); the sheet and preview were regenerated.
- Skill gap 12: `references/tools/hyperframes.md` > SVG / GSAP character rigs says "tween `strokeDashoffset` 1 -> 0" and the style file repeats it. With `pathLength="1"` that is silently rounded to 0/1 in GSAP 3.14.2 and draw-on becomes a pop-in, while `check`, `snapshot` and `inspect_video` all pass. Proposed edit: change both to `attr: { "stroke-dashoffset": ... }` (or a DrawSVG-style plugin), and add to SKILL.md step 7 "extract frames mid-animation from the MP4 and confirm partial draw-on, not only settled frames".

## Skill gaps
1. `references/styles/vintage-sketch.md` > Look (Note type): the brief asks for "handwritten" rust notes but the recipe only has EB Garamond italic, which reads as an italic caption, not handwriting. There is no vendored handwriting option. Proposed edit: add "Hand variant: a script OFL font such as `@fontsource/caveat` or `@fontsource/homemade-apple` at 40-44 px, rust, slight -1 deg rotation per note", with a note to confirm the font in a snapshot.
2. `styles/vintage-sketch.md` > Look (Line art) and Pitfalls: the "at most 40 hatch lines per plate" cap plus "no solid fills" produces light shading on one or two shapes; the result looks like clean technical line art, not engraving. The recipe never says how to make it feel engraved. Proposed edit: say what the cap is protecting (draw time) and add a recommended pattern: hatch only the shadow side of 2 shapes per plate, plus an optional second hatch set at -45 deg on the darkest shape (counts against the cap), and 3 px contour with a slightly thinner 2 px inner contour on large shapes.
3. `styles/vintage-sketch.md` > Look (Subjects): "must be simple enough to draw as paths" gives no method for drawing the kinds of subjects the style is for (a mechanism needs gears, ratchet teeth, spirals, annulus-sector shading). Proposed edit: add a short "geometry helpers" appendix (or a `scripts/` snippet) with `gearPath(n, rRoot, rTip, type)`, `spiralPath`, `sectorPath` for clip-based shading, and a hatch generator that returns one `<path>` per line (the version in `templates/vintage-sketch/src/geom.mjs` is about 100 lines and worked first time).
4. `styles/vintage-sketch.md` > Structure and Motion grammar (Notes): the plate timeline has "figure label and note (110-170)" with a single note, but the brief and the style's own variants want 2-3 notes per plate. At 190 frames per plate, three notes staggered at 66/92/118 each get 1.8-2.5 s of reading time; the recipe does not say how many notes fit. Proposed edit: "up to 3 notes per plate, appear 26 frames apart from plate frame 60, and each must stay >= `0.5 + words/3.5` s before the plate fades; minimum plate length = last note start + that time + 8 frames".
5. `styles/vintage-sketch.md` > Motion grammar (Transitions): "previous plate lines fade to 0 over 10 frames while the next title rises" puts two headlines at the same position on screen at the same time. In my first render (10-frame fade, headline rise at +5) the sheet frame at 21.9 s showed two ghosted headlines on top of each other. Proposed edit: "fade the old plate over 8 frames with an ease-out, start the next headline rise >= 8 frames after the fade starts; never overlap two headlines in the same place".
6. `styles/vintage-sketch.md` > Motion grammar (Draw on) vs `tools/hyperframes.md` > SVG / GSAP character rigs: the style says `stroke-dasharray 1`, the tool file says `pathLength="1" stroke-dasharray="1 2"`. I used `1 2`, which drew cleanly with round caps (no stray dots at offset 1). I did not test `1`, so I do not know whether it breaks. Proposed edit: make the style file use `1 2` and say why (a gap longer than the path hides the round cap at offset 1) if that is the reason.
7. `tools/hyperframes.md` > Rules / Check: (a) A chain of `tl.to()` on one SVG element (the five anchor beats, the pendulum swing) works with `tl.set(el, {rotation:0, svgOrigin})` at time 0 and `svgOrigin` repeated in each tween; the doc only shows the single-tween `svgOrigin` rule and the `immediateRender:false` rule for chained `fromTo`. Proposed edit: add this pattern with the snippet. (b) I put `data-layout-allow-overlap` on the scene sections for the crossfade, but `check` still reported `content_overlap` between two headlines at the crossfade. Proposed edit: say where the attribute must go (the text elements, or the doc says it does not apply to cross-scene overlap) after testing it. (c) `check` prints "GSAP target ... not found" warnings when a shared animation script selects classes that do not exist in a given scene; harmless, but worth one line.
8. `styles/vintage-sketch.md` > Look (Plate, Safe area): "2 px double-rule frame (inner rule 8 px inset)" does not say where the outer rule sits. The safe area is 96 px, but a double rule at 96 px would crush the plate. I put the rules at 40 px and 48 px from the frame edge and treated them as decoration outside the text safe area. Proposed edit: state "double rule 40 px from the frame edge, inner rule 8 px inside it; text safe area still 96 px".
9. `styles/vintage-sketch.md` > Look (Paper): "baked grain PNG at 10% multiply, fold, vignette" has no recipe for baking, so every agent will write its own. I did it in `scripts/bake-paper.py` (numpy + PIL, seeded, saved as JPEG at 92 quality, 0.4 MB instead of a multi-MB PNG). Proposed edit: ship that script as `skill/scripts/bake_paper.py` (reads paper and ink hex) and say JPEG is fine because it is a full-frame background.
10. `styles/vintage-sketch.md` > Format and Structure: the 25 s brief needs the 30 s structure scaled by 0.83; the recipe says only "proportions scale". I scaled title 120 -> 75, plates 225 -> 190, colophon 105 -> 100 (the colophon needs >= 90 frames to hold the stamp 1.5 s). Proposed edit: add a worked 25 s row: title 75, plates 3 x 190, colophon 105; or state minimum lengths per beat.
11. `templates/README.md` > Run a template: the table says HyperFrames needs `pnpm install` "if package.json lists dependencies"; this template has none (everything vendored, CLI via `npx`). That is consistent, but the README does not say that `render` should run the content build step first (`node scripts/build.mjs`) when `index.html` is generated. Proposed edit: add one sentence: "if `index.html` is generated, `render` and `check` must run the build script first; commit the generated `index.html` so a plain `hyperframes render` also works".

## Honest self-assessment
- Does it look like the style? Partly. The paper, the Garamond type, the double rule, the rust notes with dotted leaders and the stamp read as a printed field guide, and the line-drawing motion is calm and tactile. But the drawings look like clean vector technical diagrams, not engravings: contours are uniform 3 px, shading is a single hatched crescent per shape, and there is no tonal depth. An engraving-literate viewer would call this "sepia diagram", not "engraving". The recipe's hatch cap and "no fills" rules make it hard to do better without breaking them (gap 2).
- Mechanical content: the escapement is schematic, not accurate. The pallets are plain rectangles, not impulse faces; wheel steps half a pitch per beat but the note says "one tooth per swing" (a tooth escapes per beat; the wheel advances one tooth per full period). The anchor and wheel are not geometrically meshed; the pallets overlap tooth tips by a few pixels at rest. Good enough for an explainer, wrong for a horology audience. The pendulum has no visible link to the anchor in Fig. 3 (the note says "the anchor nudges it" and points at the bracket).
- Layout: each plate is left-weighted art plus a right column of notes with a large empty area top-right on plates 2 and 3. The recipe warns against "centered scatter layouts", which I avoided, but the composition is plain.
- Is the recipe strong enough for others to reuse? The look tokens, motion grammar and Structure table were precise and I could follow them without guessing (colors, sizes, draw-on, stamp, timing). It is not strong enough for a different subject: it gives no method for generating the drawings, which was most of the work. Another agent should be able to reproduce this template from the code in `src/`; for a new subject they would need the geometry helpers (gap 3) and the hand-lettering option (gap 1). Experimental status is fair; I would keep it experimental until a second template exists with a different subject.
- Not done: no sound (silent brief); no 4:5 variant; no field-guide scale bar; frame-by-frame diff of the drift and the swing was not measured (checked visually in sheets only).
