# Template report: audiogram-visualizer

## Result
Built and rendered with Remotion 4.0.534 (pinned). `templates/audiogram-visualizer/out/audiogram-visualizer.mp4`: 1080x1080, 30 fps, 600 frames, 20.0 s, h264 yuv420p, AAC 48 kHz. `inspect_video.py` reported OK, no edge-band warning, black 0.00 s, longest still 2.41 s at 17.6 s (the intended end-card hold; bars still breathe +-2 px). Preview: `previews/audiogram-visualizer.mp4` (0.79 MB) and `.webp` (poster at 0.46, a mid-sentence frame with wide bars and a caption). Full renders used: 2 of 3 (the first came out 601 frames because I rounded instead of floored the duration). Stills rendered per scene first (frames 20, 60, 200, 300, 400, 520, 599) and viewed. Audio is generated TTS (en-US-AndrewNeural), no music.

## Quality bar
- Bars move with loud passages and rest on quiet ones: PASS. Sheet frames at 2.5 s, 5.9 s, 9.2 s show full-width bars of different shape; frames at 0.9 s, 7.5 s, 14.2 s and 17.5 s show the 6 px idle dots (pauses between sentences and after speech).
- Captions match audio within 2 frames: PASS by construction (word times from `timeline.json`, active word 2 frames early). Not independently verified against the audio waveform; I only checked the sheet where the highlighted word agrees with the sentence position ("meeting," at 4.2 s, "hallway," at 9.2 s).
- Caption and title contrast >= 4.5:1: PASS. White `#F5F7FA` and upcoming grey `#9AA4B2` on a 70% black pill (about 7:1 or better); accent `#38BDF8` on the pill is about 9:1; title white on `#0E1117`. Cover "S" white on `#0B2A3C`-`#14587A` gradient.
- No flicker, bar change <= 40% between adjacent frames: PARTIAL. The `Debug` still measured every bar over all 600 frames: max jump 0.515 of maxH, 12 of 28,752 samples above 0.4 (0.04%). Smoothing is the 3-frame average from the recipe. The recipe does not say what the 40% is relative to (height, or maxH); I used maxH.
- Audio original, generated or licensed: PASS. Edge TTS voice en-US-AndrewNeural, script in `content.json`.
- Nothing crosses the safe area; final card held >= 1 s: PASS. Cover top y 110, caption pill bottom <= 982, bars x 112-968, progress line x 96-984; end card holds from frame 514 to 600 (2.9 s). See gaps 1 and 2 for the recipe numbers I had to change to get here.

## Skill gaps
1. `references/styles/audiogram-visualizer.md`, Look table: the stated coordinates conflict with the 96 px safe area and with each other. Cover "centered at y 250" puts its top at y 70 (inside the safe band). Progress line at y 1010 is outside the 984 bottom limit. A two-line 52 px caption needs about 140 px (2 x 52 x 1.12 + 24), not 120 (y 860-980). Bars "max height 260 centered on baseline 760" reach y 890, overlapping the caption zone. Proposed edit: replace with a consistent layout, e.g. cover center y 290, title y 490, bars baseline 700 / max 220, progress line y 826 (between bars and captions), caption pill centered y 912 (zone 842-982). This is what I built.
2. Same file, Look / Bars: "48-64 bars, width 10, gap 8" does not fit: 64 bars need 1144 px. At 1080 wide with 96 px margins only 48 bars fit (856 px). Proposed edit: "48 bars (64 only with width 8 / gap 6)".
3. Same file, Motion grammar / Bars: taking the lower 64 of 128 bins and mapping left to right with `sqrt(v) * 520` gives a lopsided wedge (measured peak 240 px on the left falling to about 30 px on the right, means 65 to 9 px). Proposed edit: say to mirror about the centre (low bins in the middle) with a mild high-frequency tilt (x1 to x2.5 across the bars) and a soft clip (`maxH * tanh(px / 130)`) instead of a hard clamp. This is what produced the balanced look.
4. Same file: no word on how to put several TTS segments into one audio file for `visualizeAudio`. The tool file's `<Sequence>`-per-segment pattern gives several mp3 files, but this style needs one continuous file. I wrote `scripts/mix.py` (ffmpeg `adelay` + `amix` + `apad`) and a `layout.json` of segment offsets. Proposed edit: add a "multi-segment narration to one wav" snippet to `audio-tts-captions.md` and mention it in the style file's Audio section.
5. `references/tools/remotion.md` / style file: no mention of `@remotion/media-utils` version pinning (needs the same exact version as `remotion`; `useAudioData` works with a wav in `public`/`Config.setPublicDir`). Also not stated that `visualizeAudio` bins 0-47 of 128 cover about 0-9 kHz and that speech sits in the lowest ~10 bins. Proposed edit: add both lines to the Pitfalls list.
6. Style file, Structure: "Outro 840-900, audio fades over the last 20 frames" assumes audio runs to the end. With narration the speech ended at 16.7 s of 20 s, so there was nothing to fade; I held a silent end card for 3.3 s. Proposed edit: "fade only if audio still plays".
7. Style file, Motion grammar / Captions: "active word in accent only if contrast >= 4.5:1" with no instruction for the spoken/upcoming word colors or how to chunk. I used the English chunking recipe from `audio-tts-captions.md` with 28-char lines (2 lines max), which worked and threw no mismatch errors, but the 28-char limit needed a balanced-split helper. Proposed edit: reference `chunk()` with `max: 28` and give an upcoming-word color (`#9AA4B2`).
8. `references/tools/remotion.md`: `duration = round(total_ms / 1000 * fps)` is easy to get one frame over a requested length; the doc uses `Math.ceil` in `calculateMetadata`. Proposed edit: tell templates with a fixed brief length to `floor` and trim the audio with `-t`.

## Tool issues
- Writing several files with heredocs in one Bash call failed once with an unmatched-quote error and nothing ran; I switched to the file tool. Not a skill issue.
- `remotion still` with `--image-format=png` was needed because stills follow the jpeg config unless the config sets png; I set png in `remotion.config.ts`, which also makes the mp4 `yuv420p` as the tool doc says.
- Render time: full 600-frame render about 34 s (parallel agents did not slow it noticeably). `pnpm install` 6 s.
- `visualizeAudio` is called 3 times per frame for smoothing and once more per `Debug` frame; the 600-frame `Debug` still takes a few seconds, fine.

## Honest self-assessment
Style fidelity: it reads as a podcast audiogram (dark ink background, accent glow, centered cover art, mirrored bars, pill captions, progress line), and it matches the recipe's intent. It is clean but plain; there is little else to see between sentences, and quiet moments show nearly invisible idle dots because the floor tone `#1F2937` is close to the background. The cover art is simple (gradient, rings, 3x3 grid, initial) and the end card is minimal. I could not compare with real audiograms (evidence is thin, per the brief), so "looks like the style" is judged against the recipe only.
Recipe strength: medium. The motion grammar (frame-indexed `visualizeAudio`, smoothing, idle floor, spring intro, 2-frame caption lead) was enough to build from and the Pitfalls were accurate. But the Look table's coordinates do not hold together (gaps 1 to 3), so another agent following it literally would get overlaps, off-screen bars or a lopsided spectrum, and would have to redo the layout like I did. With the proposed edits the recipe would be reusable. Unverified: caption sync to the audio by ear or waveform, and the flicker bar (partial). The audio itself was not listened to; its content is the Edge TTS output.
