# Template report: storybook-picture-book

## Result

Delivered. `templates/storybook-picture-book/` (HyperFrames 0.8.143, SVG + GSAP, content-driven build script), `out/storybook-picture-book.mp4` (1920x1080, 30 fps, 1189 frames = 39.63 s, h264 + aac), `previews/storybook-picture-book.mp4` (2.0 MB) and `.webp` (poster at 34.7 s, the bloom page). 2 full renders used of 3 (render 1 = deliverable, render 2 = determinism check). Per-scene stills via `snapshot` were run 3 times (about 28 s each) and viewed before the first full render. `check`: 0 errors; the warnings are `GSAP target not found` for empty selectors (for example `.puff` on pages without puffs) and Studio-timeline sub-composition hints. `inspect_video.py --frames 12`: OK, no edge-band warning, black 0 s, longest still 0 s. I viewed the contact sheet and three full-resolution MP4 frames (turn, bloom, sad).

## Skill gaps

1. `skill/references/styles/storybook-picture-book.md`, Look table (Palette) and Quality bar. The palette is "6 washes + ink" but a cloud hero, night sky, ground states (dry vs wet), stems and raindrops all need extra colours. Add: "derived colours allowed: wash darkened up to 30%, a paper-white `#FFFBF2` for clouds and highlights, and one night sky `#B9C9EA`; no other hues". Also give the dry/wet ground pair explicitly (dry = peach, wet = meadow).
2. Same file, Look table, "Page" row: "1.5% vignette" is invisible at that strength. I used 7% at the corners. State the intended visible value, or drop the vignette.
3. Same file, Motion grammar, "Page turn": it never says which page sits under the turning one, or how the clips overlap. What worked: next page's clip starts the frame the turn starts; the old clip is extended by 14 frames; earlier pages get a higher z-index; the old `.page` ends with an explicit `opacity 0` tween. Without the last step, a 2%-wide strip (38 px) of the old page stays on the left edge for the rest of the video, because the clip hides only at its end and `scaleX 0.02` is still 38 px. Add this to the recipe. Also state that page length = lead + last word + tail and that audio tags need alternating track indexes.
4. Same file, Structure: "120 frames each" and "page length = sentence audio + 0.8 s" contradict each other and do not hit "40 s". Replace with the formula I used: `page = lead 12 f + last word end + tail 12 f`, cover about 11%, end 14%, and give the resulting total for 9 lines (39.6 s at Jenny -10%).
5. `skill/references/styles/storybook-picture-book.md`, Audio: lists `en-US-AvaNeural`; the brief asked for `en-US-JennyNeural`. Add Jenny's measured pace: at -10%, 8-11 words per line took 3.8-4.6 s including about 0.9 s trailing silence (`tts.py` `duration_ms`). Also the recipe asks for a synthesized music box, but no tested synthesis recipe exists (`audio-tts-captions.md` says so). I shipped narration only. Either supply a tested ffmpeg or Python music-box snippet or mark it optional with the pad one-liner.
6. `skill/references/tools/hyperframes.md`, "SVG / GSAP character rigs": add the pattern I needed for flowers and a hanging head: rotate about a point offset from the part's own centre with `svgOrigin`, and bake geometry in absolute coordinates; avoid ancestor `transform` attributes (I generated Moko's coordinates in JS instead). Also note that `gsap.set` initial states on `fromTo(..., immediateRender:false)` chains must repeat the "from" values explicitly, or a later tween starts from the wrong pose.
7. Same tool file, "Check, then render": `GSAP target not found` console warnings from empty selectors (generated markup where a scene has no `.puff`) are harmless. Say so. Also `snapshot` prints "Describing frames with Gemini" and wrote `descriptions.md`: already documented; confirmed.
8. `skill/references/styles/storybook-picture-book.md`, Pitfalls: "Over-ambitious characters" has no example of what a 4-8 shape flower or cloud looks like. A sample shape table (cloud = 7 circles; flower = 6 petal circles + centre + 2 leaf paths + stem curve) would save the first two stills' worth of iteration. It would also help to say that a "wilted flower" built from closed petals reads as a brown pinwheel, and to give a better drawing (see self-assessment).
9. `skill/SKILL.md` step 4 / `audio-tts-captions.md`: no guidance for a book whose caption equals the narration verbatim (no `speech`/`caption` split needed). A line "if caption == speech, skip the merge helper" would help. Minor.

## Tool issues

- Hyperframes 0.8.143 `init` generates `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md`; deleted as the doc says. I kept only `hyperframes.json` (I did not need `meta.json`; `check` and `render` ran without it).
- `pnpm install` inside `templates/<id>/` worked standalone (the repo root has no workspace file).
- Render 1: 76 s for 39.6 s of video (5 workers, screenshot capture, hardware GPU), other agents rendering in parallel. Render 2: 71 s. Faster than the doc's 0.7 s per second guidance suggests would be needed with SVG filters on every frame.
- `check` reports "GSAP target not found" 10 times; harmless as above.
- No CDN, no network needed during render (GSAP and font are vendored by the build script from the pinned npm packages; woff2 is 20 KB).

## Honest self-assessment

Does it look like a picture book? Partly. It reads as a soft, cute children's story: cream paper cards, rounded caption bands, wobbly hand-drawn edges (displacement filter), a pastel palette, page curl, a single-sentence-per-page structure. It does not look like a painted watercolor book: shapes are flat vector discs with a darker edge, so the "wash" is a tint more than a pigment; the paper grain is faint. The weakest drawing is the wilted flower (closed petals hanging at the stem tip read as a brown pinwheel). The best are the cloud with its faces, the rainbow and the night page. The flowers also overlap Moko on several pages, which reads as sloppy layout; I only partially fixed it (shortened heights on pages 6-8).

Is the recipe enough for others? Mostly yes for structure, tokens and motion, which I could follow exactly. Gaps 1, 3 and 4 are what cost me time or forced guesses (palette for a white cloud, the page-turn overlap, the length formula). Gap 3's left-edge strip would hit every agent who implements the turn as written. The "EXPERIMENTAL" label is fair: one template validates it, and the recipe says nothing about how to draw a character beyond "4-8 shapes". Someone without my GSAP/SVG experience would have trouble with the hanging-head rotation and with turning the idle motions into proxy-driven code, even though the HyperFrames tool doc explains the proxy pattern.

What I did not do: no listening check of the audio (volumedetect only: mean -22.0 dB, max -2.6 dB), no music or page-turn SFX (recipe suggests both), no 9:16 variant, no check on a second machine. The recipe's "music box" and "paper-slide SFX" items are therefore not implemented, and the sound items in the Quality bar are limited to narration timing.
